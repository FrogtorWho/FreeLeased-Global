RESIDENTIAL TENANCY AGREEMENT (SAMPLE — for demo only)

1. The landlord may enter the premises at any time without notice to inspect or carry out works.

2. The tenant waives all rights to structural repair and accepts responsibility for all repairs including habitability.

3. A late fee of 50% per day applies to any rent not received by the due date.

4. The landlord may terminate this tenancy on 24 hours notice.

5. The rent may be increased at any time at the landlord's sole discretion.

6. The tenant shall pay a security deposit equal to six months rent.

7. The landlord may terminate the tenancy if the tenant makes a complaint to any authority.

8. Rent is due on the first day of each month. (This clause is lawful and should NOT be flagged.)
