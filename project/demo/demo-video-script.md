## Act 1 (0:30–1:30) — The Live Audit

**Screen:** Open the FreeLeased app → Competition tab → Live Demo subtab.

**Action:** Paste the synthetic lease text into the text area. Click "Hidden Rights Sweep" → show results appearing in real-time.

**Narration:**
> "Watch this. I paste in a lease clause — standard UK Assured Shorthold Tenancy. One click: the hidden rights sweep checks the text against 20 statutory patterns. Three rights discovered instantly. Zero inference calls. Zero compute cost."

**Action:** Click "Fairness Check" → show the flags appearing.

> "Now the fairness engine: 103 clauses analysed. Four high-severity flags raised — including an uncapped service charge and a repair clause that waives the landlord's statutory duty. Each flag carries an evidence class — established, heuristic, contested — that caps how confident we claim to be."

**Action:** Click "Consensus Gate" → show the divergent verdict.

> "The consensus gate cross-checks the codified analysis against a statute lookup. They disagree — the gate routes to human review. It never makes the decision for you."

## Technical Requirements

- **Screen recording:** Full browser window, 1080p minimum
- **Audio:** USB microphone, quiet room, no background music
- **Demo environment:** Live preview URL (must be warmed before recording)
- **Backup plan:** Pre-record the API responses as screenshots in case of cold start
- **Timing:** Hard stop at 4:00. Practice 3 times before recording.

## Common Pitfalls (from judge-panel analysis)

1. **Dead air while loading** — pre-warm all endpoints before recording
2. **Statute citations** — judges don't care about CLRA sections. Lead with the problem and the market.
3. **Too much code** — show the OUTPUT, not the code. Screenshots of results > code editors.
4. **Missing the close** — end with the URL and the ask, not "um, any questions?"
5. **Over 4 minutes** — if it's over time, cut the business section. The demo IS the pitch.
