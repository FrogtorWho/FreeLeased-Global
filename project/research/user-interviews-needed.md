## 1. Why 10, why named

- **10 conversations** gives signal saturation across the
  five user-types (UK leaseholder, UK RTM practitioner,
  Caribbean strata manager, Caribbean board secretary,
  Caribbean conveyancer).
- **Naming them** (rather than archetypes) lets us
  sequence outreach, attribute consent, and quote back.

Where we cannot name an actual human, we name the **role**
and the institution — so Sam has a concrete search query.

## 6. What we will not do

- We will not pay for interviews (cash incentives skew
  the sample).
- We will not cold-DM people who are NOT in a position
  to use the product.
- We will not record anyone who has refused recording.
