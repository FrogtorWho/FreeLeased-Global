## 6. Caveats

- **Vendor pricing pages are not stable**. Competitor pricing
  was verified on 2026-08-11. Re-verify before any customer
  claim.
- **Discounts** (annual prepay, multi-block) routinely
  reduce by 15–30%; we have not modelled discounts.
- **TCO** for SaaS includes: setup, training, audit fees,
  support. We have estimated only the headline price.
