# FreeLeased — Product & Expansion Roadmap

Anchored to the data spine's own pilot-vs-roadmap split (3 pilot jurisdictions
live, 4 more staged, then the wider Caribbean and beyond). Phases are outcomes,
not calendar promises.

## Jurisdiction expansion (the data network effect in motion)
- **Phase 0 — Pilot (now):** Barbados, Jamaica, Cayman Islands. 50 pilot
  residents. Verified registries where available; open-data spine everywhere.
- **Phase 1 — Complete the anglophone core:** Trinidad and Tobago, The Bahamas,
  Guyana, Belize. Convert drafted MoUs into registry feeds. Target: registry
  coverage from 43% to a majority of pilot+phase-1 jurisdictions.
- **Phase 2 — Wider CARICOM:** the OECS states and the remaining members. Reuse
  the ingestion and verification pipeline; marginal cost per jurisdiction falls.
- **Phase 3 — Beyond the Caribbean:** port the pattern to other small-island and
  emerging markets (Pacific, West Africa, Southeast Asia) where fragmented
  registries and mispriced climate risk look identical.

## Product roadmap
- **Now (shipped):** provenance data spine; agentic verification loop with
  evidence classes and honest abstention; Lease & Contract Fairness Check (live
  API, BB/TT rules); land-intelligence profiles per jurisdiction; telemetry hooks.
- **Next (post-reset sprint):** Fairness Check + land-intelligence in the UI;
  live demo link; OllyGarden traces in the demo; jurisdiction rules for the
  phase-1 statutes; valuation and climate-risk overlays on the parcel view.
- **Near-term:** self-hosted jurisdiction tier (data sovereignty) packaging;
  data-spine API productisation with metering; document ingestion (OCR) for
  leases at scale; comparables and automated valuation model.
- **Medium-term:** insurance-risk scoring from CCRIF + hazard bands; title-chain
  verification workflows with registries; a partner portal for agencies/lenders.

## Business milestones
- **M1:** first paying agency/lender seat in the beachhead jurisdiction.
- **M2:** first government self-hosted licence (converts an MoU into revenue and
  a registry feed).
- **M3:** data-spine API design partner among regional financial institutions.
- **M4:** three jurisdictions with live registry feeds; the network effect is
  demonstrable to investors.

## Funding logic
Bootstrapped through the sprint on sponsor infrastructure. A pre-seed raise is
justified once M1 and M2 prove both a paying commercial buyer and a government
channel. Use of funds: registry integrations (the moat) and a small
domain-expert team to de-risk the solo-founder profile.

## Risk register (live)
- Registry cooperation is slow -> open-data spine delivers value first; consumer
  tier builds goodwill.
- Data gaps in some jurisdictions -> honest abstention + coverage labelling.
- Solo-founder capacity -> advisor network now, first hires at M1/M2.
- Regulatory sensitivity -> document-only Fairness Check + full ADM controls.

## How each phase lifts the score
Scalability and PMF are two of the weakest simulated sub-criteria. This roadmap
answers both: a concrete, low-marginal-cost expansion path (scalability) and a
staged commercial + government revenue plan with named milestones (PMF).
