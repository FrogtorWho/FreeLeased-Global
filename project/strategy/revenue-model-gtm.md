# Revenue Model & GTM Strategy

## TAM / SAM / SOM (Bottom-Up)

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- **UK leaseholders**: 4.6m flats/apartments (ONS 2024) × ~2.8m leasehold households
- **Caribbean condominium owners**: ~340,000 across 9 jurisdictions (Caribbean Development Bank estimate)
- **Global leasehold/strata**: 50m+ households (Commonhold Association, FIGJAM estimate)
- **Combined TAM**: ~55m households × $120/yr average = **$6.6B ARR potential**

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
- **Year 1-2 (UK launch)**: 200,000 leaseholders actively in RTM/enfranchisement process × $120/yr = **$24M**
- **Year 2-3 (Caribbean pilot)**: 40,000 condo owners in pilot jurisdictions × $60/yr = **$2.4M**
- **Combined SAM**: **$26.4M**

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)
- **Year 1**: 5,000 UK users (2.5% of active process) × $120/yr = **$600K**
- **Year 2**: 15,000 UK + 2,000 Caribbean × blended $100/yr = **$1.7M**
- **Year 3**: 50,000 × blended $90/yr = **$4.5M**

## Policy Tailwind

### Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 (UK)
- **Royal Assent**: 24 May 2024
- **Key provisions**: Abolishes leasehold for new houses, caps ground rents at peppercorn, gives leaseholders automatic right to extend by 990 years
- **Impact on RTM**: Makes RTM more attractive as lease terms become effectively permanent. Leaseholders who previously saw RTM as temporary now see it as a long-term governance solution
- **Regulatory gap**: LFRA 2024 creates demand for digital compliance tools — but no tool exists to help leaseholders navigate the new landscape
- **Timeline**: Provisions phased in 2024-2028. Peak demand for RTM tools: 2025-2027

### Caribbean Regulatory Momentum
- **Cayman Islands**: Strata Titles Registration Act under review — government actively seeking digital governance solutions (Cayman Enterprise City MoU)
- **Barbados**: Condominium Act 1969 needs modernization — Export Barbados MoU signals government appetite
- **Jamaica**: Conveyancing (Vesting of Condominiums) Act — Jamaica National AI Task Force member (Christopher Reckord) as advisor
- **Regional**: CARICOM housing policy discussion — no digital tool exists, first-mover advantage
