## 4. First paying user — Q4 2026 target

### Conditions of the target

We will call Q4 2026 a "first paying user" success when ALL of
the following are true:

1. **A real leaseholder has subscribed to Pro tier** AND
2. **The subscription is ≥ £96 / yr** (the annual Pro price) AND
3. **The subscription was paid voluntarily** (not a co-founder,
   not an advisor, not a friend) AND
4. **The subscriber has used the product ≥ 3 times in the first
   30 days** (proves it's not a vanity purchase).

### Honest forecast

| Scenario | Probability | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|
| **Hit by 31 Dec 2026** | 35% | We have ~50 synthetic residents in the spine; converting 1 to paid is plausible but not certain |
| Slip to Q1 2027 | 40% | Most realistic; depends on whether Boardy intro lands |
| Slip to Q2 2027 | 20% | If pre-seed closes, we hire and accelerate |
| Slip beyond Q2 2027 | 5% | Would mean the unit economics don't work or the spine isn't ready |

**We do not promise a date we cannot keep.** The Q4 2026 target
is what we are aiming for; we are not claiming it has happened.

## 7. Why we publish this

Three reasons, all named:

1. **Honesty.** Investors and judges can see we have $0 revenue.
   Pretending otherwise would be the kind of lie we explicitly
   reject.
2. **Accountability.** By publishing the Q4 2026 target with
   conditions, we hold ourselves to a falsifiable claim. If Q4
   2026 passes without a paying user, this ledger will record
   that.
3. **Pre-mortem transparency.** If the Q4 2026 target slips,
   the slip is visible — not hidden in a private investor
   update.
