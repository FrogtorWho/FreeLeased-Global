### Q1. Full title, year, and authoritative text of the Act
**Finding — `confirmed (primary)`:** *Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024*, c. 22. Authoritative consolidated text published by The National Archives / legislation.gov.uk. The page states it is "up to date with all changes known to be in force on or before 05 August 2026. There are changes that may be brought into force at a future date."
**Source (primary):** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/22/contents
**Notes:** The Act has 125 sections across 9 Parts + 13 Schedules. RTM sits in **Part 3 "Other rights of long leaseholders,"** under the cross-heading **"The right to manage,"** = sections **49–52**.

### Q2. Statutory instruments / commencement regulations issued up to Aug 2026
**Finding — `confirmed (primary)`:** Commencement is staged. The RTM tranche is brought in by **SI 2025/131 (C. 6), *The Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 (Commencement No. 3) Regulations 2025***, made **6 February 2025**, under the power in s.124(3). Its own "Note as to earlier commencement regulations" lists the prior SIs:

|---|---|---|
| s.114, s.115, s.116 (Building Safety Act amendments) | 31 Oct 2024 | 2024/1018 |
| s.27 (removal of 2-year qualifying period) | 31 Jan 2025 | 2025/57 (Comm. No. 2) |
| **s.49, s.50, s.51, s.52 + s.64 (part)** | **3 Mar 2025** | **2025/131 (Comm. No. 3)** |

**Source (primary):** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/131/made
**Notes:** No later SI affecting the RTM sections was located as in force on 5 Aug 2026. (An impact assessment is referenced in the SI's explanatory note.)

### Q3. Exact commencement status of the RTM provisions
**Finding — `confirmed (primary)`:** **In force from 3 March 2025** — ss.49, 50, 51, 52, and s.64 *insofar as it inserts s.20J of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 as regards the right to manage*. Verbatim from SI 2025/131 reg. 2:
> "The following provisions of the Leasehold and Freehold Reform Act 2024 come into force on 3rd March 2025— (a) section 49 (change of non-residential limit on right to manage claims); (b) section 50 (costs of right to manage claims); (c) section 51 (compliance with obligations arising under Chapter 1 of Part 2 of the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002); (d) section 52 (no first-instance applications to the High Court in tribunal matters); (e) section 64 (restriction on recovery of non-litigation costs) insofar as it inserts section 20J of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985 as regards the right to manage."

**Source (primary):** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2025/131/made
**Notes:** These specific RTM sections are **active, not pending**. The Act's broader enfranchisement/valuation machinery (e.g. the new valuation method in ss.35–37 / Sch. 4) was **not located** among commenced provisions as at 5 Aug 2026 — treat as **uncommenced/pending** absent a later SI.

### Q4. Substantive RTM changes — non-residential threshold, costs, voting rights
**(a) Non-residential limit — `confirmed (primary)`.** Verbatim s.49 (as enacted):
> "In Schedule 6 to the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 ('the CLRA 2002'), in paragraph 1(1) (non-residential limit on right to manage claims), for '25 per cent.' substitute '50%'."

So the non-residential/commercial internal-floor-area ceiling for RTM eligibility **rises from 25% to 50%**, widening RTM to more mixed-use buildings.
**Source (primary):** https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2024/22/section/49/enacted

**(b) Legal cost recovery — `confirmed (primary)`.** **s.50** ("Costs of right to manage claims") + **s.64** inserting **s.20J LTA 1985** (as regards RTM) change who bears process costs: broadly, each party bears its own costs, curtailing landlords' recovery of RTM/non-litigation costs from leaseholders. Both are in force from 3 Mar 2025.
**Source (primary):** Act contents (ss.50, 64) + SI 2025/131 reg. 2(b),(e).
**Notes:** Detailed operative wording of s.50/s.20J not quoted here beyond the section titles confirmed at primary source; secondary summaries (Devonshires, Norton Rose) characterise the effect as removing the presumption that RTM applicants pay the landlord's costs. Tagged `confirmed (primary)` for existence/commencement, `confirmed (secondary only)` for the practical characterisation.

**(c) Voting rights — `not found`.** No change to RTM company **membership or voting rights** was located within the commenced RTM cluster (ss.49–52, s.64). The 3 Mar 2025 tranche concerns eligibility (non-residential limit), costs, compliance, and forum — **not** voting mechanics. If a voting-rights claim is being checked, it is **not supported** by the in-force LFRA 2024 RTM provisions as at 5 Aug 2026.

### Q6. Ambiguities / delayed-implementation signals in secondary sources
**Finding — `partly confirmed`, with one secondary-source discrepancy resolved at primary source:**
- The **House of Commons Library** briefing (CBP-10653) states regulations "came into force on 31 January 2025." Read against the primary SIs, **31 Jan 2025 applies to s.27 (the two-year-rule removal) via SI 2025/57**, *not* the RTM sections. The **RTM sections (49–52) commenced 3 March 2025 via SI 2025/131.** A reader could conflate the two dates — flagged.
  - Secondary: https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-10653/
- Law-firm commentaries (Devonshires 3 Mar 2025; Norton Rose 24 Feb 2025; FSP; Moore Barlow) correctly place the RTM non-residential change at 3 Mar 2025 and describe wider reforms (valuation, marriage-value removal) as **still awaiting secondary legislation** — consistent with the primary position that much of the Act is uncommenced.
**Notes:** No primary source located that contradicts the 3 Mar 2025 RTM commencement. The "delayed implementation" theme in commentary refers to the *enfranchisement/valuation* reforms, not the RTM tranche.

---

## Analytical summary
- **Strongest, checkable anchor:** s.49 changes CLRA 2002 Sch. 6 para. 1(1) from "25 per cent." to "50%", in force **3 March 2025** (SI 2025/131). Verbatim primary text obtained.
- **In force (RTM):** ss.49–52 + s.64/s.20J (as regards RTM), all 3 Mar 2025.
- **Not a voting-rights reform:** the commenced RTM cluster does not alter RTM company voting; do not assert otherwise.
- **Pending:** the Act's enfranchisement valuation reforms appear uncommenced as at 5 Aug 2026.
- **One secondary-source trap resolved:** the "31 January 2025" date belongs to s.27 (two-year rule, SI 2025/57), not RTM.
