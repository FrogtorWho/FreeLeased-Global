## 4. Procedural facts (how judging actually runs)
- **Shared rubric** across tracks; scoring compares submissions on that scorecard.
- **Two-person verification** of final scores (reduces single-judge variance →
  broad, defensible resonance beats one hero feature).
- **48-hour freeze** between final scoring and announcement (no late edits will
  count — reinforces *submit complete + demo-ready by ~Aug 14*).
- Evaluation weighs **execution, relevance, deployment potential** — which is
  exactly why the "runs in production / sovereign-edge deployable" thread and a
  working live demo matter more than surface polish.
