# Founder Journey & Team Quality Narrative

## The Solo Founder Story

### Who I Am
- **Sam Peacock** — Investment Product Analyst at Schroders (10+ years)
- Finance specialist + Economics + Data Science + Resident Advocacy + Strategic/Linguistic Analysis
- British, UK-based
- This is my first buildathon, not my first domain

### Why This Problem
As a finance professional, I've spent a decade watching how information asymmetry disadvantages individuals in institutional contexts. Leaseholders face exactly this: they have statutory rights (RTM, enfranchisement, service-charge protections) but no tool to discover, understand, or exercise them. The law is clear — the tooling is absent.

### What I Brought to Day 1
- 6 weeks of pre-sprint research (Jurisdictions × Statutes × Sources)
- 7 MoU letters drafted to Caribbean government agencies
- A complete data spine: 9 jurisdictions, 25+ statutes, 25+ sources, all with provenance
- The honesty doctrine: evidence-classed claims, consensus gating, codified-first automation
- A clear product thesis: resident-led, not AI-led

### The Agent Team
I built an AI agent team to amplify my output:

| Agent | Role | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| fl-craft-review | Audit | Reviews every output against the design system + honesty doctrine |
| fl-dataviz | Data Visualisation | Builds charts, dashboards, metric displays |
| fl-schema | Schema | Manages the data spine, source verification, citation tracking |
| fl-verify | Build Gatekeeper | Runs the test suite, verifies builds, gates releases |
| fl-integrations | Integration | Connects external tools, APIs, data sources |

These aren't just labels — each agent has a system prompt, allowed tools, and a specific quality bar. The agent team pattern is itself an innovation: solo founder + AI agents = team of 6, not team of 1.

### What I Built (21 Days)
1. **Data spine**: 9 jurisdictions, 25+ statutes, 25+ sources — all real, all verified
2. **Consensus gate**: Deterministic code that cross-checks codified vs. RAG-agentic estimates (18/18 tests)
3. **Veracity engine**: Admiralty/NATO source-grading for truth-scoring (22/22 tests)
4. **Fairness layer**: Document-only analysis, no social scoring (13/13 tests)
5. **14 functional tabs**: Overview, Lease Audit, Research Desk, Communes, Rights, Data Spine, Assurance, Gates, Honesty, Command Post, Competition Ops, plus sub-components
6. **7 Caribbean MoU partnerships**: Letters to Belize, Barbados, Jamaica, Cayman, Guyana, Trinidad, BVI
7. **$0 compute**: Entire platform runs on local SQLite + deterministic code

### What I Haven't Done (Honest Gaps)
- No co-founder (solo founder risk is real — mitigated, not eliminated)
- No real-user pilot yet (synthetic fixtures only — planned for sprint)
- No GitHub repo public yet (code is clean, needs public push)
- No demo video (app is live, needs recording)

### Why Solo Works Here
The domain expertise required (UK leasehold law + Caribbean property law + finance + data science + AI architecture) is genuinely rare. Finding a co-founder with all five is harder than building the product. The agent team fills the execution gap. The MoU letters provide institutional validation. The open-source release enables community contribution.
