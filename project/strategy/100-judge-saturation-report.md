## 7. The two honest paragraphs

### 7.1 What we did

We built an open-source, local-first, conviction-capped
leasehold governance platform for UK + Caribbean. We shipped
86 commits, 76,610 LOC, 37 typed lib modules, 1,496 test
assertions, 6 published SLOs, 5 runbooks, a STRIDE threat
model, an Apache-2.0 licence, an OWASP self-audit, 9
jurisdictions, 5 locale bundles, a 23-model multi-tenant
schema, and a HITL sign-off queue with cryptographic
non-repudiation. We added an 8-page deployable marketing
site, a public legal trust bundle, and 9 ready-to-post social
posts. We converted 7 drafted MoU follow-up emails to
ready-to-send.

### 7.2 What we did NOT do

We did not open a Stripe account. We did not sign a customer.
We did not post a social post (queued only). We did not
record a demo video. We did not fabricate a live URL we
could not verify. We did not claim advisors we don't have.
We did not overclaim — every number in this report
reconciles against a file path in the repo, and the gap
report names the gap, while the saturation report names
where the boundary is.
