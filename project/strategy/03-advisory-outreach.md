## 7. What These Emails Do NOT Do

- Do NOT commit anyone to advisory roles, paid positions, or equity
- Do NOT pressure — "no pressure" is genuine, not a social engineering tactic
- Do NOT mention other advisory targets (each outreach is confidential)
- Do NOT mention judge names or scoring mechanics
- Do NOT ask for funding, investment, or financial support
- Do NOT promise deliverables beyond "free lifetime access + acknowledgement"

## 8. Style Rules

- **UK English** throughout
- **No AI tells**: no "leveraging", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "game-changing"
- **Personalisation is mandatory**: each email must reference the person's specific work
- **Brevity**: 200-250 words email, 80-120 words LinkedIn DM
- **One ask per message**: "1-2 sentence quote by 12 Aug"
- **Professional warmth**: this is a peer request, not a supplication
