# Competitive Landscape Analysis

## Direct Competitors (UK Leasehold)

### 1. Right to Manage (RTM) Specialist Firms
- **Firms**: LEASE (gov.uk advisory), Leasehold Advisory Service, solicitors (HCB Solicitors, Pemberton Greenish)
- **What they do**: Manual, advice-based. Leaseholders pay £500-5,000+ for RTM application support
- **What they lack**: No automated statutory diagnostics. No multi-jurisdiction support. No $0 compute.
- **Our advantage**: Automated, instant, free tier, multi-jurisdiction, evidence-classed

### 2. PropTech Platforms
- **Habito** (mortgage broker): AI-driven mortgage matching, but no leasehold governance
- **Nested** (conveyancing): Digital conveyancing, but no statutory rights discovery
- **Flatfair** (deposits): Zero-deposit product, but no lease analysis
- **Our advantage**: None of them do statutory diagnostics. We're in a white-space.

### 3. Legal Tech
- **Luminance** (AI for legal review): Document review AI, but general-purpose, not leasehold-specific
- **Kira Systems**: Contract analysis, but enterprise-only, not resident-facing
- **Our advantage**: Domain-specific (leasehold/strata), resident-led, $0 compute, Caribbean focus

## Caribbean Competitors

### 4. Regional Platforms
- **No direct competitors identified** in Caribbean leasehold/condo governance
- **CARICOM housing policy**: Government-level, not resident-facing tools
- **Our advantage**: First-mover in digital leasehold governance for Caribbean

## Moat Analysis

### Defensibility Layers (ranked by strength)

1. **Data Spine** (strongest): 9 jurisdictions × 25+ statutes × 25+ sources with provenance. This is the single hardest thing to replicate — it requires domain expertise + legal research + source verification.

2. **Codified-First Architecture** (strong): Deterministic code that handles 90%+ of cases means we can run at $0 compute. Competitors using LLM-first approaches can't match this cost structure.

3. **Consensus Gate** (medium): The 2/3 human validation requirement is a design philosophy that competitors can copy but haven't — it's a cultural moat.

4. **Cryptographic Communes** (medium): k-anonymity aggregation for community-level insights. Novel but early.

5. **Provenance Chain** (medium): Every claim traceable to primary source. Trust layer.

6. **MoU Network** (weak but growing): 7 government partnerships create distribution moat. Hard to replicate in 21 days.

### What Stops a Well-Funded Competitor?
- **Time**: 21-day sprint advantage + 3 months of domain research already done
- **Jurisdiction expertise**: Caribbean legal frameworks are niche — not enough revenue for Big Law to automate
- **Cost structure**: $0 compute means we can offer free tier indefinitely; VC-backed competitors need revenue
- **Network effects**: Each lease analyzed improves the pattern library → better for next leaseholder
- **Community**: Cryptographic Communes create data network effects that compound

### What Could Kill Us?
- A well-funded competitor with existing Caribbean distribution (e.g., Sagicor, Republic Bank) building this as an add-on
- Government in-territory build: If Barbados/Jamaica build their own digital governance tool
- **Mitigation**: Open-source + MoU partnerships make FreeLeased the default, not a competitor
