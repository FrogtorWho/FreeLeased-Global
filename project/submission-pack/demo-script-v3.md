# FreeLeased — Demo Script (v3, 3 to 5 minutes)

Target: 4 minutes. Structure maps to the judging rubric (business strength + agentic AI excellence). Record screen with voiceover. Timestamps are guides.

## 0:00 to 0:30 — The problem (business)
On camera or over a Caribbean map. "Caribbean property is fragmented across thirteen jurisdictions. Records are on paper, registries do not talk, and a resident cannot even check if a lease clause is legal. That opacity blocks investment and hurts households. FreeLeased is the intelligence layer that lets the region see its property market clearly."

## 0:30 to 1:15 — The data spine (defensibility)
Show the Data Spine view. "There is no land-data API in the Caribbean, so we assembled one from public, auditable sources: OpenStreetMap, Overture, statistics offices, central banks, and the regional catastrophe-risk facility. Every cell carries its source and the time it was fetched." Hover a cell to show provenance. "This provenance-tracked, multi-jurisdiction spine is the asset competitors cannot copy by prompting a model."

## 1:15 to 2:15 — The agentic loop and honesty (agentic AI excellence)
Show the loop running on a parcel. "A multi-agent loop researches, verifies, gates, and hands off for human sign-off. Every output carries an evidence class that caps its confidence." Trigger a low-data case. "When there is not enough data, the system abstains instead of guessing. Honesty is enforced in code." Show the OllyGarden trace. "The whole loop is observable, and one kill-switch revokes every live query."

## 2:15 to 3:00 — The advocacy wedge (product utility + mission)
Open the Lease & Contract Fairness Check. Paste a lease with an unlawful deposit clause. "The same engine that values a parcel can read a lease. It flags clauses that break the law and cites the statute. It scores the clause against the law, never the person." Show a flagged clause with its citation.

## 3:00 to 3:40 — Business model and roadmap (PMF + scale)
Slide. "Pro seats for agencies, developers, and lenders. A data-spine API. A self-hosted tier for governments that keeps data inside the country. A free consumer tier that earns public trust. We expand registry by registry through MoUs already drafted for seven jurisdictions." Note beachhead jurisdiction.

## 3:40 to 4:00 — Close (team + relevance)
"Built solo, in public, entirely on the Buildathon stack: Shogo for orchestration, Impala and MiniMax for inference, Nebius for compute. The same pattern of fragmented registries and mispriced risk exists across small-island and emerging markets worldwide. FreeLeased is how a single Caribbean property market, and then many more, gets built." End on the live link.
