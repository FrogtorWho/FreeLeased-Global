# Voice & Tone — Coral

## Three things we always do

1. **Speak like a friend.** "We noticed this clause is missing a
   notice period — that might be a problem. Want us to flag it?"
2. **Use the second person.** "Your lease", "your clause", "your
   sign-off". The product belongs to the user, not us.
3. **Celebrate small wins.** "All clear! Nothing pending." "Signed
   off. Good work." The product acknowledges the user.

## Three things we never do

1. **Never be saccharine.** Coral is warm, not cloying. No emoji
   in product copy. No exclamation points in error states.
2. **Never talk down.** We are friendly, not condescending.
   Assume the reader is competent.
3. **Never hide bad news.** "Your landlord may be in breach" is
   honest; "There's a little thing we want to mention" is
   dishonest. Use plain words.

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing site | Warm invitation | "Reading your lease shouldn't feel like a fight. Let's read it together." |
| Product UI | Friendly, brief | "Clause 4 looks off. Want us to flag it?" |
| Error states | Calm, never blaming | "We couldn't read page 4 — let's try a fresh upload?" |
| Empty states | Quiet celebration | "All clear! Nothing pending. You're caught up." |
| HITL queue | Plain, audit-friendly | "Verdict: REJECT. Reason: statute mis-cited." |

## What we sound like

A knowledgeable friend who happens to know lease law. Not a
chatbot. Not a professor. Not a marketing page.

## Vocabulary do's and don'ts

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| clause, provision, covenant | term, condition, rule (jargon) |
| resident, leaseholder | tenant (wrong sub-domain) |
| flag, note, point out | identify, surface (jargon) |
| sign-off, decision | approval, review |
| verified, attested, declared | confirmed, validated |
