# Voice & Tone — Veridian

## Three things we always do

1. **Lead with the resident, not the regulation.** "Your landlord must
   give 24 hours' notice" beats "Section 21(1)(a) of the Housing Act
   1988 requires..."
2. **Use plain English for the conclusion, technical English for the
   citation.** "This clause is one-sided" + "s.27(3)(a) Landlord and
   Tenant Act 1985". The reader never has to translate.
3. **Show our work — but only when asked.** The default UI hides
   provenance behind a `?` toggle. The deep-dive view exposes it.

## Three things we never do

1. **Never catastrophise.** No "you could lose your home" framing. We
   surface risks; we don't weaponise them.
2. **Never moralise about landlords.** Some are excellent. Some are
   not. The system flags clauses, not people.
3. **Never imply certainty where evidence is partial.** If our
   evidence class is "weak", we say "based on the documents you've
   shared, this *appears to be*..." — never "this is".

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing site | Warm, confident, invitational | "Read your lease like a lawyer — without the law degree." |
| Product UI | Calm, precise, brief | "Evidence: weak (3 sources). Sign-off required." |
| Error states | Direct, never blaming | "We couldn't read page 4. Re-upload?" |
| Empty states | Curious, low-pressure | "Nothing pending. You're caught up." |
| HITL queue | Professional, audit-friendly | "Verdict: REJECT. Reason: statute mis-cited." |
| Press | Crisp, factual, no superlatives | "FreeLeased ships with 40+ verified UK statutes and zero compute spend." |

## What we sound like

A senior conveyancer explaining things to a friend over coffee. Not
a chatbot. Not a professor. The friend who's done this 200 times.

## Vocabulary do's and don'ts

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| resident, leaseholder | tenant (wrong sub-domain), customer, user |
| clause, provision | term, condition, rule |
| sign-off, decision | approval, vote |
| evidence class | score, rating (we don't score people) |
| statute citation | law (vague) |
| verified, attested, declared | confirmed (we don't confirm) |
