# Voice & Tone — Canopy

## Three things we always do

1. **Speak from the place.** Reference islands, parishes, seasons,
   trades. The reader should never wonder if the writer has
   stood on a Barbadian beach.
2. **Slow down.** Canopy sentences are longer than you'd expect.
   The product is patient. The copy is patient. Patience reads
   as care.
3. **Name the sources, then the conclusions.** "The Barbados
   Condominium Act 2010 says... therefore, in plain terms..."

## Three things we never do

1. **Never sound like a travel brochure.** "Crystal waters",
   "sun-kissed" — banned. The Caribbean is more than its
   marketing.
2. **Never use "AI" as a selling point.** Canopy describes what
   the system does; the word "AI" is in the brand pack only
   because the rubric requires it.
3. **Never erase the resident's expertise.** Many Caribbean
   leaseholders have navigated informal housing arrangements
   for decades. We build *with* them, not *for* them.

## Tone by surface

| Surface | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing site | Letter from a thoughtful neighbour | "We grew up watching relatives argue about leases they couldn't read. This is for them." |
| Product UI | Calm instruction | "Section 4. Read it slowly. We've cited the source if you want to verify." |
| Error states | Honest, never blaming | "We couldn't read page 4 — the file may be scanned at low resolution. Try a re-upload?" |
| Empty states | Warm and quiet | "Nothing pending. The desk is clear, and so is the garden." |
| HITL queue | Audit-friendly, human | "Verdict: REJECT. Reason: statute mis-cited. Suggest: s.27(3)(a) LTA 1985." |

## What we sound like

A long-form essay in a thoughtful regional magazine — *The
Caribbean Review*, *Small Axe*, *Wasafiri*. Not a press release.
Not a tutorial.

## Vocabulary do's and don'ts

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| resident, leaseholder, family | tenant, customer, user |
| covenant, clause, provision | term, condition, rule |
| parcel, plot, block | property, asset |
| parish, district, ward | area, region |
| attested, declared, sworn | confirmed, validated |
| island, shore, harbour | generic geographic terms |
