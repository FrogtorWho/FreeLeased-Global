## Assured Shorthold Tenancy Agreement

**Date:** 1 March 2025

### Parties

**Landlord:** Pinnacle Management Ltd
Registered in England & Wales, Company No. 12345678
Address: Unit 7, Riverside Business Centre, London SE1 2BN

**Tenant:** Alex Morgan
Address (prior): 14 Maple Road, Brighton BN1 4JX

**Property:** Flat 4, 27 Chestnut Grove, London SW19 8BH
(Basement floor conversion, 1 bedroom, 1 bathroom)

**Term:** 12 months commencing 1 March 2025 and ending 28 February 2026

**Rent:** £1,850.00 per calendar month, payable in advance on the 1st of each month by standing order.

---

### Clause 1 — Rent and Payment

1.1 The Tenant shall pay the Rent monthly in advance on the first day of each calendar month by standing order to the Landlord's nominated account.

1.2 Late payment shall attract interest at 3% above the Bank of England base rate, calculated from the date payment was due until the date payment is received.

1.3 The Landlord may increase the Rent annually by agreement or, failing agreement, in accordance with the provisions of the Rent Act 1977 or the Housing Act 1988, as applicable.

---

### Clause 2 — Deposit

2.1 The Tenant shall pay a deposit of £5,550.00 (equivalent to three months' rent) upon signing this Agreement.

2.2 The deposit shall be held by the Landlord as security for the Tenant's obligations under this Agreement, including but not limited to: damage, unpaid rent, dilapidations, and professional cleaning costs.

2.3 The deposit shall be returned within 21 working days of the end of the tenancy, less any deductions properly made.

---

### Clause 3 — Service Charge

3.1 In addition to the Rent, the Tenant shall pay a monthly service charge of £275.00 to cover: building insurance, communal area maintenance, lifts, drainage, external repairs, and management fees.

3.2 The Landlord reserves the right to increase the service charge at any time to reflect actual costs incurred in managing and maintaining the building. The Landlord shall provide 30 days' written notice of any increase.

3.3 The service charge is not subject to consultation with leaseholders or tenants and is determined solely at the Landlord's discretion based on reasonable expenditure.

3.4 The Tenant shall not be entitled to an itemised breakdown of service charge expenditure unless such a request is made in writing.

---

### Clause 4 — Repairs and Maintenance

4.1 The Tenant shall keep the interior of the Property in good and tenantable repair and shall, at the end of the tenancy, yield up the Property in the same condition as at the commencement of the tenancy, fair wear and tear excepted.

4.2 The Tenant shall be responsible for all internal repairs including plumbing, electrical fixtures, appliances, locks, and decoration.

4.3 The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs and external maintenance, including the roof, external walls, and communal areas.

4.4 The Tenant shall notify the Landlord within 24 hours of becoming aware of any defect, damage, or disrepair.

---

### Clause 5 — Use of Property

5.1 The Property shall be used as a private residence for the Tenant and, with the Landlord's prior written consent, no more than one additional occupant.

5.2 The Tenant shall not use the Property for any commercial, illegal, or immoral purpose.

5.3 The Tenant shall not cause or permit any nuisance, annoyance, or disturbance to the neighbours or other occupants of the building.

5.4 Smoking is prohibited within the Property and all communal areas.

---

### Clause 6 — Assignment and Subletting

6.1 The Tenant shall not assign, sublet, part with possession, or share occupation of the Property or any part thereof without the prior written consent of the Landlord, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld.

6.2 The Landlord may withhold consent at its sole discretion where the proposed subtenant or assignee fails to meet the Landlord's referencing criteria, including credit check, employment verification, and right-to-rent check.

6.3 Any subletting without consent shall be a material breach of this Agreement and entitle the Landlord to terminate immediately.

---

### Clause 7 — Access

7.1 The Landlord, or its authorised agents, may enter the Property at reasonable times upon giving 24 hours' written notice to carry out inspections, repairs, or to show prospective tenants or buyers.

7.2 In cases of emergency, the Landlord may enter the Property without notice to prevent or mitigate damage to the Property or neighbouring properties.

7.3 The Tenant shall permit the Landlord or its agents access to the Property at any time if required by law or for the purposes of carrying out essential repairs.

---

### Clause 8 — Notice to Quit

8.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving not less than two months' written notice expiring on the last day of a rental period.

8.2 The Landlord may terminate this Agreement by service of a Section 21 notice under the Housing Act 1988, provided the notice requirements have been satisfied.

8.3 The Landlord may re-enter the Property and terminate this Agreement immediately if:
- (a) the Rent or any part thereof remains unpaid for 14 days after becoming due;
- (b) the Tenant commits a material breach of any obligation under this Agreement;
- (c) the Tenant becomes bankrupt or enters into a voluntary arrangement with creditors;
- (d) the Property is used for illegal purposes.

---

### Clause 9 — Forfeiture

9.1 The Landlord may re-enter the Property at any time if the Rent is unpaid for 14 days (whether formally demanded or not) or if the Tenant commits a breach of this Agreement.

9.2 Upon re-entry, the tenancy shall be absolutely determined but without prejudice to any right of action of the Landlord against the Tenant in respect of any antecedent breach.

9.3 The Landlord shall not be liable for any loss or damage to the Tenant's property arising from the exercise of the right of re-entry.

---

### Clause 10 — Utilities and Council Tax

10.1 The Tenant shall be responsible for the payment of all utilities (gas, electricity, water, sewerage, broadband, television licence) from the commencement of the tenancy.

10.2 The Tenant shall be responsible for Council Tax from the commencement of the tenancy.

10.3 The Landlord shall be responsible for any ground rent, if applicable.

---

### Clause 11 — Building Safety Act 2022

11.1 The Landlord confirms that the building complies with the Building Safety Act 2022 requirements applicable to higher-risk buildings, where applicable.

11.2 The Landlord shall provide the Tenant with a copy of the building safety case and fire risk assessment upon request.

---

### Clause 12 — Right to Manage

12.1 The Tenant is advised that the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002 provides qualifying leaseholders with the right to take over the management of their building through the Right to Manage (RTM) process (sections 79–96).

12.2 This Agreement does not affect the Tenant's statutory rights under the Commonhold and Leasehold Reform Act 2002, the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, or any other enactment.

---

### Clause 13 — Governing Law

13.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

13.2 Any disputes arising under this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.
