Pricing

Free for the resident. £12/mo Pro. Institutional, custom.

We charge a flat fee so a single leaseholder in a £400 service-charge dispute pays less than a £3,000 audit. Pro tier is for the resident who wants unlimited dossiers. Institutional is for housing associations, RTM companies, condo-management agencies.

Free

£0/mo

For the single leaseholder with a single dispute.

  • Single user, single device
  • Local-first by default (your lease never leaves the browser)
  • Up to 100 dossiers / month
  • 30-day dossier retention
  • Community support

Use the GitHub repo →

Pro

£12/mo

For the resident in a long dispute or tribunal-adjacent process.

  • Up to 5 users
  • Cloud LLM analysis (consent-gated, audit-logged)
  • 1,000 dossiers / month
  • Sign-off queue with ed25519 signatures
  • 3-year dossier retention
  • 3-day support response SLA

What's not in here

  • No data brokerage. We do not sell to insurers, lenders, agents.
  • No per-dossier fees. The £12 is the price.
  • No contractually-binding £3,000 "audit" upcharge.
  • No urgency / scarcity / "your lease expires tomorrow" dark patterns.

The not-legal-advice disclaimer

FreeLeased is a software platform. It is not a law firm. We do not give legal advice. For any decision that could materially affect your legal rights, engage a qualified lawyer admitted in the jurisdiction. The "Engage a local attorney" nudge is non-removable at high severity.
