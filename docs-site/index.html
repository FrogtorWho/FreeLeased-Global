A leaseholder who can read their own lease is asymmetric.

FreeLeased is an open-source, local-first platform that reads a UK or Caribbean lease, compares it against statute, and surfaces every meaningful disagreement — with sources, with confidence intervals, and with the human sign-off a tribunal will respect.

The asymmetry thesis

Existing property tools — agent CRM, block-management SaaS, audit services — are built for the gatekeeper, not the resident. They optimise the gatekeeper's revenue. We optimise the resident's legibility: the ability to read what's there, know what it means, and act on the difference.

That's a small wedge. But in a market where £2.3B+ of UK service charges are contested every year, and Caribbean condo boards can levy hurricane rebuilds without audit, "the resident can see" is asymmetric.

Two markets, one engine

UK leasehold is 4.5M units, with 70% of service charges contested at least once in the property's lifetime. The Caribbean has 1.2M strata units under Barbados Condominium Act 1985, Trinidad Condominium Act 1975, Jamaica Condominiums Act, Cayman strata law, and Belize / Guyana / BVI equivalents. The FreeLeased engine ships the same conviction-capped, HITL-gated dossier for both.

UK

UK Leasehold Reform Act 2022. s.20 consultation. s.20C costs. s.72 CLRA Right-to-Manage. BSA 2022 + EWS1 fire-safety. s.167 CLRA forfeiture. Service-charge audit.

Caribbean

Barbados, Jamaica, Trinidad & Tobago, Cayman Islands, Belize, Guyana, British Virgin Islands. Civil-law hybrids where applicable. Hurricane-rebuild consent decrees.

What this is not

  • Not a property-management CRM. We are resident-facing.
  • Not a SaaS dashboard. Apache-2.0 source. Self-host if you must.
  • Not a legal-advice product. The "Engage a local attorney" nudge is non-removable at high severity.
  • Not silent on AI. Every claim shows conviction (0.33 / 0.60 / 0.75 / 0.99). HITL on every high-severity claim.
