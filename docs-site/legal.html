Privacy Policy

Mirrors docs/PRIVACY.md.

TL;DR

FreeLeased is local-first. Your lease uploads are processed on your device, or — only where you opt in — on our EU/UK-hosted infrastructure. We never sell data. We never train third-party models on your data. The legally binding text follows.

1. Data controller

The data controller is Sam Peacock ("Founder"), sam.peacock1@gmail.com. If you self-host, you are the data controller and the Founder is not a joint controller.

2. What we collect

2.1 Lease documents and case data

  1. Local browser (default — file content never leaves your device).
  2. Cloud LLM endpoint (only if you click "Analyse with cloud LLM").
  3. OllyGarden trace endpoint (sampled metadata only — no file content, no pseudonym).

2.2 Account data

Email, display name, org/tenant ID. Retained until you delete + 30 days.

2.3 Telemetry

Anonymous page-views, crash reports (stack-trace only), and LLM spans (jurisdiction, severity — never claim text). Opt-out in Settings → Privacy.

2.4 What we never collect

Biometric data, financial account credentials, special-category data under Art. 9, children's data.

3. Lawful basis (UK GDPR Art. 6)

4. Sub-processors (current)

Vercel · Supabase · OllyGarden · Nebius AI Studio · Giotto.ai · MiniMax. Full list with DPAs at docs/PRIVACY.md §6.

5. Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection, withdraw-consent. Email sam.peacock1@gmail.com with subject "DSR — ". 30-day SLA. In-product Settings → Privacy covers the common ones.

6. International transfers

EEA/UK → US transfers rely on SCCs. The Caribbean sovereign-edge path actually avoids most transfers.

7. Retention

7 years for sign-off audit (UK limitation period); 30-day raw OTLP; 30-day encrypted backups. Anonymous telemetry: 90 days aggregated.

8. Complaints

ICO (UK), national EU supervisory authority, or your Caribbean DPA. Email us first at sam.peacock1@gmail.com subject "Privacy complaint".
