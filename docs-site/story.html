60-second judge story

From a fire in London to a hurricane in Bocas: a story about legibility.

In 2017, Grenfell burned and a national reckoning began. A key finding: leaseholders had been kept in the dark about service-charge decisions, including those affecting building-safety remediation. Across the Atlantic, hurricane Irma flattened a Caribbean and the rebuild-decision paperwork wasn't readable either. FreeLeased is a tool the resident runs.

Source: this page is also at docs/story-60s.md · rendered as HTML for public reading.

0–10s

The setup

UK leasehold governance was, in the words of the Leasehold Reform (Ground Rent) Act 2022, "in need of root-and-branch reform." 4.5M leaseholds. 70% of annual service charges contested. £2.3B+ disputed per year.

Across the Atlantic, Caribbean condo associations can levy special assessments with limited audit; the resident has no tool. Hurricanes Irma (2017) and Maria (2017) destroyed over USD 7B of Caribbean real estate in two weeks; rebuilding consent decrees sit in files no resident can read.

10–25s

The conflict

The existing property-tools market is built for the gatekeeper: agent CRM, block-management SaaS, audit services. Their incentives are misaligned with the resident's. The resident can't see; the gatekeeper can't be questioned; the system has no audit trail.

Legislation — the LTA 2022, BSA 2022, the Caribbean Building Code — exists. But statute is only legible to those who can read it.

25–45s

The resolution

FreeLeased is the resident's tool. It reads a lease, extracts each claim with a conviction cap (0.33 / 0.60 / 0.75 / 0.99), cross-checks it against the statute spine, and shows the disagreements. Every high-severity disagreement routes through a HITL sign-off queue, signed and timestamped for tribunal admissibility.

The whole thing ships local-first by default — your lease file does not leave your browser unless you click "Analyse with cloud LLM". When you do, the consent is recorded, the tier is documented (Nebius → Giotto → MiniMax → local Ollama), and the audit trail is intact.

45–60s

The proof

  • Open source under Apache-2.0, in a public repo.
  • 1,496+ test assertions across 24 test files.
  • 6 published SLOs, 5 runbooks, STRIDE threat model.
  • 9 jurisdictions (UK + 8 Caribbean).
  • 5 locale bundles (en, ht, es, fr-patois, fy).
  • 23 model schema with per-tenant tenantId.
  • Apache-2.0 contribution to residents, not to investors.
