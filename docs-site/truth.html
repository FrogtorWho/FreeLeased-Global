Where the lease disagrees with statute, we surface it.

The FreeLeased Truth-Diff reads a lease, extracts each claim with a conviction cap (0.33 / 0.60 / 0.75 / 0.99), and lines up the lease's wording next to the statute's wording, with a verdict and a HITL sign-off queue. The excerpts below are public sample inputs; the live running app processes them on-device by default.

Lease clause 4.2

"The Tenant shall pay a service charge comprising the cost of all works to the Building as the Landlord in its absolute discretion deems necessary."

s.19(1) LTA 1985

Service charges are payable only if "reasonable", and recoverable costs are limited to those specifically incurred. The landlord's discretion is constrained.

CONFLICT · 0.99

"Absolute discretion" over broad scope is not a lawful basis. Recommend: demand itemised accounts + reasonableness challenge under s.27A LTA 1985. Engage a solicitor.

Lease clause 6.1

"The Tenant shall not assign or underlet without the prior written consent of the Landlord, such consent not to be unreasonably withheld."

s.19(1A) LTA 1985 (added LFRA 2024)

Landlord must respond to consent request within 28 days; deemed consent if silent; cannot impose conditions beyond statute.

PARTIAL · 0.75

Lease wording still lawful but new statutory protections apply even where the lease is silent. Recommend: serve a formal consent request, time-box 28 days.

Lease clause 9.4

"Ground rent: £250 per annum, doubling every 10 years."

LTA 2022 (Ground Rent) Act

For new leases, ground rent must be a "peppercorn" (zero). For existing leases, the doubling clause is unenforceable on demand. Eligible tenants can apply to the Tribunal to vary.

CONFLICT · 0.99

If lease dated after 30 June 2022, the doubling clause has no effect. If earlier, vary it via s.19 LTA 1985 (as amended). High severity: engage a solicitor.

Caribbean condo declaration § 11

"The Council may levy a special assessment for emergency works, with the amount to be determined by the Council's surveyor."

Barbados Condominium Act 1985, s.22

Special assessments require notice to all unit-owners + 14-day review; the amount must be reasonable + supported by invoices. "Council's surveyor" alone is insufficient.

CONFLICT · 0.85

If your building received an emergency assessment post-hurricane without a 14-day review window, the levy may be challengeable.

Lease clause 11.3

"The Tenant shall maintain the Building in good repair."

s.11 LTA 1985 (implied repairing obligation, landlord's side)

Structural and common-part repair is the LANDLORD's implied obligation, not the tenant's. The lease as drafted wrongly inverts this.

CONFLICT · 0.99

If you are billed for structural works, this is a textbook s.11 claim. Caveat: specialist counsel for lease-specific carve-outs.

How the conviction cap works

Every claim is tagged with one of four conviction levels: 0.99 (statute-anchored) 0.75 (well-sourced) 0.60 (probable) 0.33 (unanchored LLM)

Anything at 0.33 requires HITL sign-off before it appears in a published dossier. Anything at 0.99 cites the source statute.

The non-removable "Engage a local attorney" nudge

Why this nudge is permanent. FreeLeased gives you information, never legal advice. At high severity, a non-removable banner nudges the user toward a qualified lawyer admitted in the jurisdiction. We will not ship a version of this banner that is behind a paywall or that can be dismissed without a deliberate click.
