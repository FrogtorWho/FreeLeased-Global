The team

One founder, three advisors in flight, and a Discord.

FreeLeased is a solo project for now. As we approach the pre-seed close (Q4 2026 target), we expect to add an engineer and a GTM hire. The advisory board is forming.

Sam Peacock — Founder

Sam Peacock is an Investment Product Analyst at Schroders, the UK-listed asset manager, where he has spent 10+ years across investment analytics, product governance, ESG, and quantitative risk.

He holds degrees in Finance and Economics and a postgraduate specialisation in Data Science. He is a UK-resident; he has family roots in Jamaica.

He started FreeLeased after the Grenfell-tower inquiry's findings on service-charge opacity made clear that the asymmetry between leaseholder and gatekeeper had no tool on the resident's side. He is the only person in this commit history for now; the [`AGENTS.md`](https://github.com/sam-peacock/FreeLeased-Global/blob/main/AGENTS.md) file lists the overnight AI agent ("Shogo") which writes code-reviews and docs under his direction.

Sam is reachable at sam.peacock1@gmail.com and on Discord as FrogtorWho.

Advisory board (in formation)

The five target seats:

  1. UK leasehold law (solicitor) — statutory accuracy, sign-off culture
  2. Caribbean bar (barrister-at-law) — cross-jurisdiction, multi-sovereign
  3. Privacy / DPO — UK GDPR + Caribbean DPA compliance
  4. Open-source governance (e.g. former CNCF / Apache) — open-source maturity
  5. Insurance / property economics — Insurtech lane, lender integrations

All seats are honorary for the first 12 months. See project/management/RACI.md §4.
