Pilot onboarding

Want to pilot FreeLeased? Here's how.

We run real pilots with real leaseholders and real estate professionals. Below is the procedure we follow — from the first email to the published case study. Everything is consent-gated; you can stop at any time.

Step 1 — Reach out

Email sam.peacock1@gmail.com with the subject Pilot. We aim to reply within 3 business days. If you don't hear from us in 7, please reach out via the FreeLeased Discord (FrogtorWho).

Step 2 — Discovery call (60 min)

A 60-minute video call with the founder. We do not pitch. We listen. The script we use is public at project/strategy/customer-discovery-script.md, and includes:

  • Pre-amble: confirm consent, attribution choice, recording choice
  • Six open-ended questions (real experience, numbers, incidents)
  • Closing: clean transcript + thank-you note

Step 3 — Consent form

You sign a consent form ([project/pilot-audit/consent-template.md](https://github.com/sam-peacock/FreeLeased-Global/blob/main/project/pilot-audit/consent-template.md)) before we share any documents in app. You can choose:

  • Named attribution (we credit you on the case study)
  • Role-only attribution (e.g., "RTM director, West Yorkshire")
  • Anonymous attribution (your wording, no role)

Step 4 — Pilot session (30–60 min)

A real leaseholder (or institutional operator) uses FreeLeased on their own lease file. The session is screen-shared with consent. We don't coach; we observe. Notes go straight into an anonymised transcript.

  • Default: local-first mode (lease never leaves the browser)
  • Optional: cloud-LLM tier with consent (audit-logged)
  • Recording: only if you consent; transcription is opt-in

Step 5 — Dossier + sign-off

You get a per-claim dossier with conviction anchors, statute references, and a HITL sign-off queue. If you're satisfied, we ship the dossier export. If you have any concern, we revert to draft and discuss.

Step 6 — Optional public case study

After the pilot, you choose whether to publish a public case study on this site. We never publish without explicit attribution consent. We also offer:

  • Co-authored blog post
  • Conference talk co-presentation
  • Advisory board invitation (for repeat partners)

Data protection commitments

Zero PII by default. Pilot data on FreeLeased is local-first by default. We do not upload your lease to any third-party LLM without consent. We pseudonymise any datasets that would be shared for research. See Privacy Policy §2.3 for details.
